ABOUT

One person, one project at a time.

Blueprint is a one-person studio, which means the person who designs your site is the same person who answers your emails.

B.

I'm Douglas Bridge, founder of Blueprint.

I started Blueprint after noticing that most smaller businesses I visit online have old, outdated websites. That makes sense — most don't want the cost and time-sink of a large corporate web designer, and are wary of the deposits many smaller designers require. That's where Blueprint comes in.

Blueprint is a one-person studio, completely run by me — you'll only ever communicate with the person actually designing your site, and since I only work on one project at a time, you'll always be the priority. No deposit is required, and no charges are applied until you're completely satisfied with the result, so there's zero risk to you.

A bit of background: I'm a bioengineering graduate and full-time rugby player, with a self-taught passion for Python, HTML and JavaScript. Based in Richmond, I visit plenty of smaller businesses, which is what prompted me to offer a service that helps them improve their online presence — and their number of customers.
