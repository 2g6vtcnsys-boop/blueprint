About

A small team out of Astley.

Design and build across Worcestershire and the counties either side of it.

We are based at Solhampton Farm, just outside Astley, and we work across Worcestershire and the counties either side of it.

The same people do the survey, the build and the handover. If you ring the number on the van, you get someone who has been on your job and knows what stage it is at.

We take on the whole thing rather than a slice of it — groundwork, structure, carpentry, cladding, glazing, paving and the internal finish. That is partly preference and partly practical: the stages that usually slip are the ones handed to somebody else's diary.

Why the photographs look like this

Every picture on this site is a job we did, taken on site, on a phone, usually with something still half-finished in shot. None of it is stock and none of it is a manufacturer's catalogue. If a photograph here looks like a brochure, it is because the job did.
