Canopies & carports

Our own canopy system, made in the UK.

A self-supporting aluminium lean-to with the drainage hidden inside the eaves beam — no bulky downpipes.

The AVR canopy is a complete self-supporting glazing system built from structural aluminium profiles, manufactured here in the UK. The aluminium and the powder coating together are what give it the strength and the life; the detail that sets it apart is the drainage.

Integrated drainage

There are no bulky drain pipes. The drainage runs inside the eaves beam, which is why the canopy reads as a clean line rather than a structure with plumbing attached to it.

Bespoke sizes

Designed and tested to take any width, with an additional post every four metres. If your opening is an awkward size, that is the normal case rather than the exception.

Glazing

16mm multiwall polycarbonate, clear or opal. High impact resistance, and light transmittance that keeps the space underneath usable rather than gloomy.
