Cladding & carpentry

Battens out, and then the part you actually see.

Cedar and composite cladding, structural and finish carpentry, doors, windows and porches.

Cladding is mostly carpentry. What decides whether an elevation still looks right in five years is the batten grid behind it, the ventilation gap, and how the corners and reveals are cut — none of which you can see once the boards are on.

Cedar

Western red cedar, with the grain and the colour movement that comes with it. It silvers if you leave it and holds its tone if you treat it. Either is a decision, not an accident, and we will tell you which you are choosing.

Composite

Wood-grain composite board in charcoal, grey, natural, teak or brown. It does not rot, warp or need treating, and it holds its colour. On a north elevation or anywhere awkward to re-treat, it is usually the better call.

Doors, windows & porches

Replacement windows and doors in UPVC, aluminium or timber, and the making-good around them that decides whether the job looks finished. We fit composite front doors, aluminium sliders and bi-folds, French doors, and build the porches over them.

  • Composite, UPVC, aluminium and timber
  • Sliders, bi-folds, French and patio doors
  • Porches, canopies and side entrances
  • Full making-good: reveals, cills, plaster and trim

If you are replacing windows as part of a wider renovation, doing it in the same programme as the cladding or the render saves scaffolding twice.
