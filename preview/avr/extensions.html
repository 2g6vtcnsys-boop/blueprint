Extensions & renovations

More room, and the rest of the house still makes sense.

Single and double-storey extensions, structural openings, whole-house renovations and full internal remodels.

An extension is the point where a build either disappears into the house or announces itself for the next thirty years. We spend the design stage on the join — roof line, brick, window proportion, floor level — because that is what decides which one you get.

What we take on

  • Single and double-storey rear and side extensions
  • Structural openings, steels and knock-throughs
  • Whole-house renovations and internal remodels
  • Period properties — barns, cottages and Victorian brick
  • Garages, annexes and outdoor buildings

We handle the groundwork ourselves, which matters more than it sounds: foundations, drainage and levels are where an extension's programme usually slips, and they are the part most builders subcontract.

How it runs

From first visit to the day you get the keys back.

Four stages, and you always know which one you are in.

01

Survey

We come out, measure up and talk through what is possible and roughly what it costs. Free, no obligation.

02

Design & quote

Drawings where they are needed, building control where it applies, and an itemised quote you sign off.

03

Build

Groundwork, structure, envelope, first and second fix. One team, tidy at the end of every day.

04

Handover

Snagged, cleaned and finished, on the date agreed at the quote.
