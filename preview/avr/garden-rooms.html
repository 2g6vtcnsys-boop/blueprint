Garden rooms

A room at the bottom of the garden, usable in February.

Bespoke, fully insulated, plastered and painted — and normally permitted development, so no planning application.

Four things people ask

The answers, before you have to ask them.

01

Bespoke made

Built to your space and your budget rather than to a catalogue size, so it fits the garden you actually have.

02

Fully insulated

Internally plastered, painted and insulated, so it is a room you use all year, not a shed you use in July.

03

Planning permission

Not normally required — our garden rooms fall within permitted development. We will tell you if yours is the exception.

04

Build & installation

In most cases we pre-manufacture the building, which cuts the time we spend on site and in your garden.

What people use them for

Most start as one thing and end up as another. The build is the same either way; what changes is the power, the insulation and what goes on the walls.

  • Home office and studio space
  • Acoustic studios
  • Annexe and guest space
  • Gym
  • Garden bar and entertaining space
  • Garages and secure storage

Interiors are finished like a room in the house — we have done oak slat feature walls, polished microcement, and a spa room built around a hot tub.
