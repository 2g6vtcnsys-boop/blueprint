Decking, paving & driveways

Everything under it, then everything on it.

Composite and hardwood decking, porcelain patios, screen walls, block paving and the groundwork underneath.

A patio or a driveway fails from underneath. The visible half is a day or two of the job; the rest is excavation, sub-base, falls and drainage, and that is what decides whether it is still level in five winters.

Composite decking & cladding

WPC composite board, supplied and fitted. No treating, no rot, no warping, and it keeps its colour. Available in black, grey, natural, teak and brown, in a woodgrain finish with narrow spaced grooves.

  • Anti-slip surface
  • UV and weather stable
  • Does not rot or warp
  • Made from eco-friendly materials
  • Virtually maintenance free

Porcelain paving

Porcelain patios, paths and side returns, laid on a full bed with cut lines kept honest and a channel drain where the falls need one.

Garden design

If the whole garden is coming up, it is usually cheaper and always tidier to do it as one job: levels, drainage, paving, deck, screen and lawn in one programme rather than four visits from three trades.

We do both traditional and contemporary layouts, and we are happy to work to a garden designer's drawing if you already have one.
